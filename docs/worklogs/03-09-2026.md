# 03-09-2026 — ADR-008: preferences fold into `users`, and the route to production

> Plan: [[preferences-v3-plan]] — supersedes [[user-preferences-plan]] and
> [[character-lite-optimization-plan]]. Yesterday's work: [[01-09-2026]].

## Summary

Design review of the two plans merged on 01/09, then the reversals it produced.
Neither feature could have worked outside localhost; that is the half of this
that is not a design change.

Nothing was deployed when the reversals were decided — every commit involved sat
on `feat/user-preferences`, and neither `origin/main` nor `origin/release`
contained any of them. That is the whole reason for doing them now: they cost a
migration and a fast-forward rather than a rollback. It all went to production at
the end of the same session; see **Deployed to production** below.

Roles were swapped for this session at the Owner's instruction — K implemented
rather than handing the plan to N.

## ADR-008

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Drop `user_preferences`; one `users.preferences JSONB` column | Strictly 1:1. `users` already had the RLS policy and grants (007), a row created in five code paths, and `DELETE FROM users` in `db/gdpr.py`. The second table bought a second policy, a JOIN, a seed step that made GET write, and a third copy of `display_name` |
| D2 | Everything in the JSONB — no typed columns | Preferences are an open set. A typed column per setting is a migration per setting; a field in `SyncedPrefs` is neither |
| D3 | No foreign key on `selected_character_slug` | The catalog soft-deletes, so `ON DELETE SET NULL` never fires, and `slug` is stable so `ON UPDATE CASCADE` buys nothing. The app-level check is strictly stronger than the FK was |
| D4 | No colour enum anywhere but `avatarPalette.ts` | Was three places (CHECK constraint, Pydantic `Literal`, palette). Frontend and backend deploy independently, so a colour released to the UI first would 422 for whoever picked it |
| D5 | Drop the `version` optimistic lock; last write wins | The merge is per key. Two devices changing two different preferences never disagreed, and the 409's resolution was always "take the other write" |
| D6 | PHI guard is `extra="forbid"`, not a keyword blocklist | The blocklist checked five names at the top level only. `{"a": {"injury_history": …}}` and `{"note": "thoát vị L4"}` both passed |
| D7 | Restore `SELECT 1 FROM characters WHERE slug=$1 AND is_active` | After D3 this is the only guard, and it was always the only one that could see `is_active` |
| D8 | `theme` and `graphics.*` stay device-local | Owner's call, reaffirmed. Q3 answered it for graphics; the FOUC fix stays inside that boundary |
| D9 | `display_name` leaves the preferences API | Cognito owns it — `ProfileContent.tsx` reads `custom:displayName` from the token. `users.display_name` and `user_preferences.display_name` were copies nobody read |
| D10 | Keep `/me/preferences`, keep two serial requests on load | The URL maps to one column. The next step is a 9-17 MB VRM download, so a second round trip is noise, and `/characters/{slug}` keeps its public 5-minute cache |

Q4 in the v2 plan is recorded as "chốt" but the Owner never answered it — §2 of
that plan says so and §3 promotes the inference to a decision anyway. D8 is that
question actually being asked and answered.

## Changes

### Schema
- `alembic/versions/010_preferences_into_users.py` — new. Adds
  `users.preferences JSONB`, carries 009's rows across with
  `jsonb_strip_nulls(jsonb_build_object(...)) || p.prefs`, drops the policy,
  index and table. `downgrade()` rebuilds 009 and unpacks the column back,
  coalescing any colour 009's CHECK would reject.

### Backend
- `api/schemas.py` — `SyncedPrefs` with `extra="forbid"` replaces the
  `AvatarBg` Literal and the flat fields. `UserPreferencesOut`/`Patch` carry
  `preferences` and nothing else.
- `api/routes_preferences.py` — rewritten. `_validate_prefs`,
  `ALLOWED_AVATAR_BG`, the size/depth/prototype limits and the PHI blocklist are
  all gone; the model does that work. GET is one SELECT and creates nothing.
  PATCH is one UPDATE with `preferences || $2::jsonb`, guarded by the `is_active`
  check, with the string-matched `"foreign key"` handler removed.

  read, so one stale row cannot 500 for one user.

# Session pointer: written when there is something to point at, and expiring

Second piece of work, prompted by a 404 the Owner spotted in the console while
checking the above.

## The fault

Every clean page load sent one request that could only ever 404:

```
localStorage empty
  → loadOrCreateSessionId() minted a UUID and stored it immediately
  → the mount effect asked GET /sessions/{the id it had just invented}
  → 404
```

The `conversations` row is written server-side on the first turn
(`db/session_store.py`, `INSERT … ON CONFLICT (session_id) DO UPDATE`), so the
gap between "a pointer exists" and "a row exists" always answered 404. Pressing
**new chat** and walking away stored an id no message ever followed — and that
one 404'd on every load from then on, permanently.

Confirmed against Neon rather than assumed: `19c6ccde-25ed-401c-ac34-9f1f15c62f26`
sat in that browser's localStorage and **existed in `conversations` for no user
at all**, while the account's seven real conversations were untouched.

`ChatContext.tsx` already knew — `if (status !== 404) console.warn(...)` — but
swallowing it in the app does not silence the browser's own network log. The
only way to stop the noise is to not send the request.

And the pointer had no expiry of any kind: localStorage does not expire, and
nothing in the app cleared this key, not even on sign-out.

## Decisions (ADR-009)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | Write the pointer **late** — only when a message is sent | Closes the window the 404 lived in. No pointer, no request |
| S2 | **TTL of 2 hours**, re-stamped on every send | Owner asked for 1–2h; the upper end so lunch or a meeting still resumes. The clock runs from the last message, not from the start, so an active conversation never expires mid-use |
| S3 | TTL is **client-side** | It governs whether a refresh *resumes*, not whether a conversation can be *opened*. Picking an old one out of the sidebar must keep working however old it is, so `GET /sessions/{id}` is untouched |
| S4 | Keep auto-resume rather than drop it | Losing your place on an accidental F5 is a real regression; that is what the mechanism is for |
| S5 | Discard the legacy bare-string value | Its age is unknowable and it may be one of the pointers that never had a row — exactly the case found. Nothing is deployed and there are six dev users, so the cost is one lost resume, once |
| S6 | Clear the pointer on sign-out | A pointer into account A's conversation was surviving into account B. Harmless only because `GET /sessions/{id}` filters by the token's user and answered 404 — the noise being removed |

No backend change. The server still accepts a client-minted id and creates the
row on the first turn.

## Changes

- `src/lib/chatSession.ts` — new. `readSessionPointer` / `stampSessionPointer` /
  `clearSessionPointer`, with `now` and `storage` as parameters so the rules test
  without a DOM (vitest runs in the `node` environment; jsdom is not installed
  and adding it would touch the pinned lockfile). Storage is `{"id","at"}`;
  expiry is enforced on read — lazy, the way Redis expires most keys — because
  localStorage will never do it for us.
- `src/contexts/ChatContext.tsx` — `loadOrCreateSessionId` gone (it was being
  called **twice** in render). `sessionIdRef` and `activeSessionId` are now
  `string | null`. The mount effect returns early when there is no pointer,
  **calling `setIsRestoring(false)` on the way out** — that flag is cleared in a
  `finally`, so returning without it would leave it true forever and the greeting
  effect, which waits on it, would never run. `startNewSession` clears the
  pointer instead of minting one. The single send site calls `ensureSessionId()`,
  now the only place an id is created.
- `src/hooks/useChat.ts`, `src/components/panels/ChatSessionsPanel.tsx` — the
  widened type. The `s.session_id === activeSessionId` comparison needed no
  change: `null` matches nothing, so no row highlights when there is no
  conversation, which is correct.
- `src/components/AuthGuard.tsx` — `clearSessionPointer()` alongside the Amplify
  key purge.

## Things worth knowing next time

**`deploy.ps1` does not work as committed.** It never passes
`-c agent_image_tag`, and `app.py` constructs `VvaAgentStack` unconditionally, so
synthesis raises for *every* stack in the list — the script fails before it
deploys anything. Two context values have to be supplied by hand, and neither is
written down anywhere:

```
-c agent_image_tag=1f2ca773f181c212a04eaaa0e0d680a24456a056   # the live image; read it back with
                                                              # aws lambda get-function --function-name vva-agent --query Code.ImageUri
-c motion_key_pair_id=KGUIRRSF1V55H                           # aws cloudfront list-public-keys
```

Getting the image tag wrong is not a harmless mistake — `agent_stack.py` says a
deploy that omits it "would DELETE the live one, and CloudFormation would call
that a success". Passing the tag the live function already runs makes
`VvaAgentStack` a no-op, which is what was done here.

**`--exclusively` was required.** `VvaAssetStack` refuses to synthesize without
`-c motion_public_key_file=motion_signing_key.pub`, and that file exists neither
in the repo nor on this machine — so it comes into any command that pulls it in
as a dependency, including a plain `cdk diff` of the three changed stacks.
`--exclusively` narrows the selection and also guarantees the agent and asset
stacks are not touched at all.

**Amplify builds `release`.** Found from `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` on the deployed
characters function, which lists `https://release.d32nf9wwqqt016.amplifyapp.com`
— and `deploy.ps1` has said so in its closing message all along ("set it as
VITE_API_GATEWAY_URL on the Amplify 'release' branch"), which was read only
afterwards. That is the answer to "which branch is production", and it is why
the ordering above mattered.

**A new API Gateway route takes a minute or two to propagate.** Immediately after
the deploy, `/me/preferences` still answered `Missing Authentication Token` even
though `get-resources` listed the resource and all three methods. It was
answering `Unauthorized` a couple of minutes later with nothing changed in
between. Worth not diagnosing as a deployment race, which is what it looks like.

**VvaCharacterStack carried an undeployed change from 20-08.** `d4781fe` removed
the `"*"` CORS wildcard from `layer/shared/response.py` and had never been
deployed — the characters function was last updated 20-08. Checked before
deploying rather than after: `infra/infra/origins.py` and the function's live
`ALLOWED_ORIGINS` both already contain the production Amplify origin, so the
tightening had nothing to break. Confirmed afterwards from the response headers.

## deploy.ps1, fixed

It now discovers both context values instead of asking anyone to remember them:
the agent image tag from the function that is running
(`aws lambda get-function --function-name vva-agent`), and the key pair id from
`aws cloudfront list-public-keys`. Reading the tag back is the safe default by
construction — the value it finds is the one already deployed, so including
VvaAgentStack is a no-op, and the failure mode agent_stack.py warns about
(deploying a tag that is not there, and deleting the live function) needs a
deliberate `-AgentImageTag` to reach.

Every stack now deploys `--exclusively`, so a run touches only what is listed and
no dependency drags VvaAssetStack in. VvaAssetStack itself is skipped with an
explanation unless `-MotionPublicKeyFile` is passed, since the .pub is not in the
repository — better than failing the whole run on a stack nobody asked about.

`./deploy.ps1 -WhatIf -SkipBuild` now reports **no differences** on all three
stacks, which is also an independent check that what was deployed today matches
the repository.

The docstring's warning that VvaVpcStack and VvaKimodoEcsStack were stuck in
UPDATE_ROLLBACK_COMPLETE is gone: all seven stacks read UPDATE_COMPLETE.

# 03-09-2026 (chiều) — Tách ngôn ngữ, Phase 1: locale của frontend

## Vấn đề

Hệ thống lẫn tiếng Việt và tiếng Anh vì **hai trục khác nhau đang bị gộp làm một**.
Owner phân định:

- **Locale** = ngôn ngữ của *trang web*. UI chrome. User chọn.
- **Ngôn ngữ hội thoại** = hỏi tiếng gì trả lời tiếng đó. Theo từng lượt, **không dính locale**.

Hai trục được phép lệch nhau: web tiếng Anh + gõ tiếng Việt = UI Anh, trả lời Việt.

Minh hoạ cô đọng nhất nằm gọn trong một dòng — `ChatPanel.tsx:81` chứa đồng thời
`'Đang tải hội thoại trước...'` và `'Online · Ready to chat'`. Không ai cố ý; chỉ là
không có gì chặn.

## Vì sao dùng i18next chứ không tự viết module

Bản nháp đầu chọn tự viết, dựa trên con số sai: chỉ đếm chuỗi *tiếng Việt* (~40).
Nhưng khi có công tắc locale thì mọi chuỗi người dùng thấy đều phải dịch, kể cả chuỗi
tiếng Anh hardcode. Đo lại: ~47 JSX text + ~34 attribute + ~40 chuỗi Việt, trên **53 file**.

Runtime thì đơn giản thật — **không plural, không format ngày/số, chỉ 2-3 chỗ nội suy**.
Xét riêng runtime, module tự viết ~60 dòng là đủ. Nhưng rủi ro không nằm ở runtime,
nằm ở **độ phủ và khả năng tái phát**. Thứ thật sự mua được là
`eslint-plugin-i18next/no-literal-string` — **fail build** khi có chuỗi hardcode mới

| UI chrome, greeting, `ui_strings` nhân vật | **locale** |
| **Safety template** | **locale** (trước: `detect_lang(final_answer)`) |
| **Persona overlay** (voice, examples) | **locale** |
| TTS voice | `detect_lang` |

**`shared/lang.py` giờ chỉ còn đúng một nơi dùng: `voice.py`.** Đúng như Owner nói mấy
vòng trước — chỗ duy nhất cần đoán là giọng đọc, vì nó phải khớp ngôn ngữ của *âm thanh
phát ra*, mà cái đó do model quyết chứ không do user khai.

**Đánh đổi Owner đã chấp nhận, ghi lại để sau này không ai tưởng là bug:** UI để English
+ user gõ tiếng Việt → câu trả lời **tiếng Việt**, cảnh báo an toàn **tiếng Anh**.

## Đã làm

**Persona: 7 file phẳng → 4 thư mục × 3 file.**
`personas/<slug>/{_core,vi,en}.md`. Core (tiếng Anh) giữ *nhân vật là ai và được làm gì*;
overlay giữ *cách nói*. Không phải bản dịch — `"Xưng 'mình', gọi người dùng là 'bạn'"`
không có tương đương tiếng Anh, đó chính là lý do phải tách.

**Xoá `eca_default`, `eca_clinical`, `eca_friendly`.** Còn 4 nhân vật thật, khớp đúng 4
slug active trong DB. Mặc định khi client không gửi `persona_id` → `anne` (8 chỗ hardcode).

**Bỏ `_fallback_persona()`.** Persona hỏng → `PersonaError`. Trước đó nó trả một nhân vật
"ECA Default" giả cho mọi slug sai: user vẫn nhìn avatar Bronya trong khi đọc lời của một
nhân vật không tồn tại.

**Loader: gộp lúc lưu, dàn phẳng lúc đọc.** `_persona_cache[slug]` giữ **khoá chuỗi** và
chứa cả hai ngôn ngữ; `get_persona(slug, lang)` dàn phẳng khi đọc. Nhờ vậy
`build_persona_prompt`, `build_voice_card`, `get_safety_text` **không phải sửa một dòng**,
và `test_a0_persona_security.py` — vốn truy cập `_persona_cache["..."]` bằng khoá chuỗi để
chứng minh id xấu không đầu độc cache — không bị ép đổi hình dạng vì một tính năng.

**`locale` chạy từ frontend xuống:** `useLocale()` → `ChatRequest.locale` →
`config.configurable` → synthesizer / grader / main. Grader **bỏ hẳn `detect_lang`**.

**`error_handler` thôi phụ thuộc persona.** Nó chạy khi thứ gì đó đã hỏng, và persona
không load được là một trong những thứ nó có thể đang phải báo cáo — nên giờ nó dùng bảng
text trung tính theo locale. `get_ui_string` nuốt `PersonaError` vì mọi caller của nó đều
đã ở trên đường lỗi.

**Frontend:** `characterCopy.ts` đọc `ui_strings[locale]`; xoá `AUTHORED_LOCALE` — hằng số
đó tồn tại *chỉ để đánh dấu trạng thái tạm của Phase 1*, nên nó biến mất cùng lúc.
Xuống cấp có chủ đích của Phase 1 đã gỡ: **user tiếng Anh nhận lại giọng nhân vật.**

## Còn nợ

- **Chưa kiểm end-to-end trên UI.** Cần `python start_services.py --only docker backend
  frontend`, rồi: UI **Tiếng Việt** phải y hệt hôm nay (hồi quy quan trọng nhất);
  UI **English** + gõ tiếng Anh → greeting **có giọng nhân vật**, không một chữ Việt nào;
  `persona_id` không tồn tại → **lỗi rõ ràng**, không phải nhân vật khác trả lời.
- **`sync_personas_to_db.py` chưa chạy thật** (mới `--dry-run`). Docstring của chính nó
  cảnh báo row DB **che** file markdown, nên shape sai sẽ hỏng âm thầm.
- **Bản nháp tiếng Anh của 4 nhân vật cần Owner duyệt giọng** — đây là tài sản sản phẩm,
  không phải code. `anne/en.md` viết tay; ba nhân vật kia được *thêm mới* phần
  `## Voice` và `## Examples` (trước đó chúng không có), suy ra từ các quy tắc đặc thù
  ngôn ngữ vốn nằm nhầm chỗ trong `## Behavioral Rules`.
- **`pytest.ini` thiếu `ignore::PendingDeprecationWarning`** — vẫn chưa vá, và nó vừa làm
  tôi mất một vòng.
